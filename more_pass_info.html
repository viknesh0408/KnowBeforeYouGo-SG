Singapore Work Passes & Entry Options

Choose the right visa for your purpose. Singapore offers different passes for workers, professionals, students, families, and tourists.

Semi-Skilled Workers

Work Permit

For semi-skilled foreign workers in construction, manufacturing, marine, services, and process sectors.

  • Sector-specific eligibility rules apply
  • Employer applies on your behalf via MOM
  • Typically processed within 1–3 working days
  • Must carry pass card on you at all times
  • Levy and quota restrictions may apply
Learn more on MOM
Mid-Skilled

S Pass

For mid-skilled foreign employees with relevant qualifications and meeting salary criteria.

  • Typical salary band around SGD 2,500–4,999/month
  • Employer quota and levies apply
  • Requires relevant qualifications and work experience
  • Issued for 1–2 years and renewable
  • Employer applies via MOM’s portal
Learn more on MOM
Professionals

Employment Pass

For professionals, managers and executives with qualifying salaries and credentials.

  • Typical min. qualifying salary from ~SGD 5,000 (higher for experienced applicants)
  • Employer-sponsored; employer applies on your behalf
  • Valid 1–2 years and renewable
  • May lead to Dependant’s Pass for family members
  • Assessment considers qualifications, experience and sector
Learn more on MOM
Tech Specialists

Tech.Pass

For established tech professionals, leaders and specialists supporting Singapore’s tech ecosystem.

  • Merit-based pass for tech experts (no fixed salary floor)
  • Allows multiple employers/ engagements within validity
  • Valid for 1–3 years depending on assessment
  • Requires demonstrable track record in technology
  • Apply via MOM; targeted to boost local tech capabilities
Learn more on MOM
Business

EntrePass

For foreign entrepreneurs who want to start and operate an innovative business in Singapore.

  • Company must be registered with ACRA and show business plans
  • Evaluation based on innovation, investment and market potential
  • Valid initially 1 year and renewable based on business milestones
  • May require evidence of funding or revenue
  • Apply via MOM’s online system
Learn more on MOM
Students

Student Pass

Required for foreign students enrolled in full-time courses at approved institutions in Singapore.

  • Issued to full-time students at schools/polytechnics/universities
  • Apply via the Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) or school
  • Valid for duration of the course; renewals as needed
  • Limited part-time work allowed under conditions
  • Medical insurance may be required for some students
Learn more on ICA
Family

Dependant's Pass

For spouses and children of eligible Employment Pass or S Pass holders residing in Singapore.

  • Issued to eligible family members of pass holders
  • Validity tied to main pass holder’s pass duration
  • Spouses may require work passes to be employed
  • Children may attend local schools
  • Apply through MOM after main pass approval
Learn more on MOM
Long-term Stays

Long-Term Visit Pass

For family members or long-term visitors who need stays of several months up to a year.

  • Issued for 3, 6 or 12 months depending on circumstances
  • No work permitted under this pass
  • Sponsored by a local resident or relative in Singapore
  • Renewable subject to approval
  • Apply through ICA
Learn more on ICA
Tourists

Tourist Visa / No Visa

Many nationalities can enter Singapore visa-free for short visits; others require a tourist visa.

  • Visa-free entry for many nationalities (30–90 days)
  • Cannot work while on visitor entry
  • Check ICA visa requirements by nationality
  • May require proof of onward travel and funds
  • Apply for visas via ICA if needed
Learn more on ICA
