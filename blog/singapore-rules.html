Singapore Work Pass Rules You Must Know

Published on: 20 Jan 2025 | KnowBeforeYouGo SG

Singapore has strict employment laws to protect both employers and foreign workers. Before entering Singapore, you must clearly understand your work pass conditions.

1. Work Only in Approved Occupation

You must work only in the occupation mentioned in your IPA. Working in a different role is a serious offence.

2. Salary Must Match IPA

Your employer must pay the salary stated in your work pass approval.

3. No Work Before Pass Issued

Working before your pass is issued is illegal, even if employer asks.

⚠️ Breaking work pass rules can lead to fines, jail or deportation.
