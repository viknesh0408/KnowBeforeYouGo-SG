Complete Work Pass Checklist: Before You Fly to Singapore

Essential documents, verification steps, and things to check before boarding your flight.

Blog Index

Preparing for your first trip to Singapore for work? Don't board that flight without checking these essential items. A small mistake can cost you your job before you even arrive.

1. Verify Your IPA Document

Your In-Principal Approval (IPA) is the most important document. Before you fly:

  • Check that your name matches exactly as shown in your passport
  • Verify the employer name matches what was promised
  • Confirm the job title is what you agreed to
  • Check the salary amount matches your offer letter
Red Flag: If any detail doesn't match, contact your employer immediately. Don't fly hoping it'll be "fixed later."

2. Required Documents Checklist

Pack these in your carry-on luggage (not checked baggage):

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • IPA letter (printed copy)
  • Offer letter from employer
  • Ticket booking confirmation
  • Proof of accommodation address
  • Recent passport-sized photos (2-4 copies)

3. Research Your Employer

Before flying, spend $5.50 on ACRA BizFile to verify the company exists and check its details. Look for:

  • Company registration number
  • Registered address
  • Directors' names
  • Business activity

Visit: bizfile.gov.sg

4. Know the Agent Fee Rules

Important: Agents can only charge 1 month's salary for a 1-year pass, or 2 months for a 2-year pass. Anything more is illegal. Get receipts for all payments.

5. Emergency Contacts

Save these before you fly:

  • MOM Helpline: 6438 5122
  • Your employer's local contact number
  • Known accommodation address in Singapore

Final Check Before Leaving

Ask yourself:

  • Does my passport have at least 6 months validity?
  • Have I printed my IPA letter?
  • Do I know my exact employer and job title?
  • Has my employer confirmed accommodation?

If anything feels wrong, call MOM before you fly. It's better to delay than to arrive and face problems.
