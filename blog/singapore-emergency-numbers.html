Emergency Numbers You Must Save in Singapore

Police, ambulance, MOM hotline - all emergency contacts every worker should have.

Save these numbers in your phone now. You never know when you'll need them.

🚨

Police / Ambulance

999

Free call. For crimes in progress, medical emergencies, fires.

🚑

Non-Emergency Ambulance

995

For medical emergencies. SGD 95 applies (waived for SC).

💼

MOM Helpline

6438 5122

Work pass issues, employer disputes, general enquiries.

🏢

MOM Online

mom.gov.sg

Check pass status, submit feedback, find forms.

🏥

Singapore General Hospital

6222 3322

24/7 Accident & Emergency. Outram Rd.

🏥

National University Hospital

6779 5555

24/7 A&E. 5 Lower Kent Ridge Rd.

📞

ICA Enquiries

6391 6100

Visa, immigration, passcard issues.

🔧

SP Group (Power)

1800 778 8822

Power outages, electrical issues.

Important Notes

  • 999 - Free from any phone. Police will respond to crimes in progress.
  • 995 - SGD 95 fee for non-Singapore citizens (may be covered by employer or insurance).
  • MOM 6438 5122 - Available Mon-Fri 8:30am-5:30pm. For work pass issues, they can help with disputes, overpayment recovery, and general advice.
💡 Tip: Save your employer's local contact number too. If you get injured at work, your employer must help you get medical attention.
