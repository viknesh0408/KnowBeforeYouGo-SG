Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the name, email, phone number and message you submit are sent through Formspree to our inbox, used only to reply to you.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data (pages viewed, device type, approximate location) using cookies.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies to show ads, including personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

Your choices

  • Manage or turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.
  • Block analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or clear cookies in your browser.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. You can ask us to delete your message at any time.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
